Keyboard Shortcuts

The essentials — every shortcut that saves a technician minutes per day, in four compact tables.

Every shortcut here was checked against Microsoft's, Google's and Mozilla's current documentation. Context matters: F2, F5 and Ctrl+Shift+N do different things inside File Explorer than elsewhere.

ShortcutAction

Note: in PowerShell, curl and wget are aliases for Invoke-WebRequest — use curl.exe when you want the real thing. And in PowerShell 5.1/7.x, history is easier through Get-History than the old F7 popup.
