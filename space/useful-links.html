Useful Links

From SterrenKids to the VVS: the Dutch and Belgian websites this chapter keeps coming back to.

Most of this chapter is about NASA's side of the story — but some of the best astronomy on the web is written closer to home, in Dutch, on both sides of the border. These are the links this chapter recommends: for kids, for learning, for the latest news and for looking up.

🧒

SterrenKids

Astronomy for children in Dutch: clear explanations, games, craft ideas and events from two observatories.

Visit site → 🎓

LeerAstronomie

Dutch astronomy for beginners, kids and schools — articles, activities, learning materials and an online academy.

Visit site → 📡

Astronomie.nl

The Dutch astronomy portal of the NOVA Informatiecentrum: news, sky events, telescopes and organisations.

Visit site → 🇧🇪

Astronomie.be

The astronomy portal for Flanders — free brochures, observatories, clubs and amateur astronomers (AstroLAB IRIS).

Visit site → 🗂️

Astronomie.start.be

A link directory: telescopes, magazines, observatories, planets, spaceflight and much more.

Visit site → 🌌

Frank Deboosere

The Belgian weatherman's site — weather, the night sky, ISS passes, quizzes and a daily podcast.

Visit site → 🔭

VVS

The Vereniging voor Sterrenkunde, founded 1944: 2,300 members, local clubs, working groups and the magazine Heelal.

Visit site →
