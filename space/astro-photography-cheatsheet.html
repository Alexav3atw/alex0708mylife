Post-processing in 4 lines

  1. PIPP — NEF/JPEG in → tab Planetary (planets) or Solar/Lunar Full Disc (Moon) → centres, crops, sorts → AVI/TIFF out
  2. AutoStakkert!3 — stack best 50% (Moon) / 10–20% (Jupiter) / 30–50% (Saturn) · Drizzle 3× for planets → 16-bit TIFF
  3. RegiStax 6 — Wavelets: layers 1–3 carefully + Denoise → Do All
  4. NX Studio / GIMP — exposure, colour, polish

Save into: fotos\maan\ or fotos\planeten\ → RAW / gestackt / bewerkt
