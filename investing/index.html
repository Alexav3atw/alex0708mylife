Buffett indicator

The classic gauge of overall market valuation: the total value of all US-listed companies (the Wilshire 5000 Full Cap index, which tracks that value in billions) divided by the country's yearly output (nominal GDP, quarterly). Above 100 % the stock market is worth more than a full year of US economic output — a rough “is the market expensive or cheap?” thermometer. Data: Yahoo Finance and FRED (St. Louis Fed), both without API keys.

How this works

The page is rebuilt automatically by a script in the repository (scripts/update_investing.py, scheduled in .github/workflows/investing.yml), the same pattern as the Space news page. If a data source fails, the last known values stay on the page. Colours show the daily change and the one-week / one-month trend: green up, red down, grey unchanged — the arrow and sign always carry the meaning too. Nothing here is financial advice; it is a personal watchlist.
