Recent advice

A twice-a-month, stock-by-stock review of the watchlist — one verdict per company, with the reasoning and sources.

Reviewed 25 Nov 2026 · next review 1 Dec 2026. Each company gets one of five verdicts — keep, reduce, add, review or sell — based on the latest reports, prices and news at the time of the review. The verdicts are the author's personal view of his own watchlist; nothing here is financial advice, and positions, weights and amounts are deliberately not shown.

Keep Reduce Add Review Sell

Warehouses Estates Belgium WEB.BR

Reduce
  • Dividend flat since FY2023 (€3.35) while the payout ratio runs at ~94% — above the company's own 90% target.
  • H1 2026 rental income fell 3.4%; the largest tenant (Ikanbi, 4.6% of rent) terminated early.
  • Trades at ~0.69x EPRA NAV with a ~8.9% yield — the discount is the market pricing the risk, not a free lunch.
  • Small cap with limited liquidity and a Wallonia-only portfolio: the review recommends a smaller position.
Sources WEB H1 2026 report WEB FY2025 annual report

Care Property Invest CPINV.BR

Keep
  • Yield ~7.7%, dividend unchanged at €1.00; FY2026 EPS guidance raised (4 Nov 2026).
  • Debt ratio sits at ~49% against the 50% RREC ceiling — little headroom, and a Dec 2025 capital increase shows dilution risk.
  • Korian care sites integrated, Apricusa sites re-let. Keep, but watch the balance sheet, not just the yield.
Sources CPINV Q3 2026 report De Tijd — Apricusa resolution

Xior Student Housing XIOR.BR

Review
  • Cheapest of the Belgian REITs: ~0.63x EPRA NTA, yield ~7.6%, dividend raised +4%, occupancy 98.5%.
  • LTV 51.3% — above its own <50% target; divestments promised, none signed yet.
  • Review: either a conviction position or not at all — a token stake does nothing.
Sources Xior 9M 2026 release Xior FY2025 release

Ascencio ASCE.BR

Keep
  • Narrowest discount of the group (~0.80x NTA), yield ~8.5% maintained; lowest-cost balance sheet (cost of debt 2.25%, LTV 44.8%).
  • Portfolio ~9.7% over-rented — expect negative reversion; Carrefour holds break options on 4.4% of rent.
  • FY2025/26 results published 25 Nov 2026 after the close — check them before acting.
Sources Ascencio H1 FY2025/26 report Ascencio Q3 FY2025/26

Aedifica (ex-Cofinimmo) AED.BR

Keep
  • Cofinimmo was absorbed by Aedifica on 1 July 2026 (1.17842 AED per COFB share; COFB delisted) — holders should verify their broker statement shows Aedifica.
  • Healthcare real estate is the most defensive REIT sub-sector: indexed, state-linked rents; LTV 42.2%, ~90% hedged.
  • €16m+ merger synergies targeted by 2027; dividend raised +5% (€4.95 per former COFB share).
Sources DH — merger completed, 1 Jul 2026 Aedifica H1 2026 slides

NN Group NN.AS

Reduce
  • Strong fundamentals: ~9.5x forward earnings, ~5% yield, dividend +12.8% (2026 interim +12%), Solvency II 224%, €350m annual buyback.
  • The reduce verdict is a diversification call (one more rate-sensitive European financial), not a quality call.
  • CEO succession underway (Bosklopper from Mar 2027) — routine, but watch the first strategic update.
Sources NN H1 2026 results NN CEO succession

Shell SHELL.AS

Reduce
  • 8.2x trailing earnings, dividend raised +9% y/y, new $5bn buyback (to Jan 2027), gearing 16.6%.
  • Q3 was driven by record refining margins ($42/bbl vs $24 in Q2) — exactly the cyclical number that mean-reverts.
  • Reduce is a sizing call on a pure commodity-cycle bet; the cash machine itself is in good shape.
Sources Shell Q3 2026 results Shell buyback announcement

BYD 1211.HK

Reduce
  • H-shares at 14.3x trailing / 11x forward — cheap next to the Shenzhen A-share line (~22x).
  • Q3 profit +28%; October sales 490k units (best since Dec 2024); Hungary production started; exclusive talks for the Ineos Hambach plant in France.
  • But: China volume −28% YTD (price war), gross margin −0.9pp QoQ, exports fell two months running, the EU minimum-price undertaking is not yet confirmed, and a Dutch court barred the Ti7 model in the EU.
  • Reduce toward a calmer single-stock size; keep only the part you'd buy today.
Sources Bitauto — BYD October sales MarketScreener — BYD/Ineos Hambach

Groupe Bruxelles Lambert GBLB.BR

Keep
  • ~20% discount to NAV (€99.21 at 30 Sep 2026); dividend €5.125 (~6.5% yield, intended stable).
  • Aggressive structural buybacks (ninth €500m envelope approved; €888m returned YTD) — the mechanism that actually closes discounts.
  • Recordati take-private completed 24 Nov 2026; core book of quality (SGS, Affidea, Imerys, Pernod Ricard).
Sources GBL 9M 2026 results GBL — Recordati closing

KBC Ancora KBCA.BR

Keep
  • Discount to its KBC stake narrowed from ~31.5% (Jun) to ~19% — much of the easy re-rating has happened.
  • KBC Group underneath: 9M profit +11%, RoTE 18%, CET1 14.6%; KBCA dividend +23% to €4.32 (4.9% yield).
  • A single-asset vehicle (18.6% of KBC) — keep it as the Belgian bank exposure, not as a diversifier.
Sources KBC Ancora FY2025/26 results KBC Group Q3 2026

Sofina SOF.BR

Keep
  • NAV discount ~17% (it was ~25–30% in Sep–Oct); H1 NAV +6.8%.
  • The underlying book is venture capital in disguise: ByteDance largest direct holding; funds hold SpaceX, Stripe, Anthropic, OpenAI.
  • Yield 1.35% — this is a growth position, not an income one.
Sources Sofina H1 2026 results Sofina half-year report 2026

Berkshire Hathaway BRK-B

Keep
  • 16.1x trailing earnings, 1.61x book; record $377B cash; record $9.6B quarterly buybacks under CEO Greg Abel.
  • Watch item: insurance underwriting weakening (GEICO pretax −45%); net seller of equities 15 of the last 16 quarters.
  • The only internally diversified, non-European position on the list — keep as core.
Sources Reuters — Berkshire Q3 AInvest — $377B cash, $9.6B buybacks

Xiaomi 1810.HK

Review
  • Q3 (24 Nov): revenue −5.2%, adjusted profit −57%, gross margin 20.2% (−2.7pp) on the DRAM memory-cost shock; smartphone shipments −29% in a −22% market.
  • EV business still loss-making (~RMB 3.4B operating loss) at ~100k deliveries a quarter; Europe entry only in 2027.
  • Review: the phone thesis is broken near-term and the EV thesis is unproven — hold only as a declared 2027-Europe bet.
Sources MarketScreener — Xiaomi Q3 ZonaMovilidad — Europe 2027

C3.ai AI

Sell
  • Revenue −25% YoY and still guiding a $123–155M annual operating loss; Tom Siebel back as CEO, ~26% of staff cut.
  • Merger talks with Automation Anywhere (Jan 2026) never confirmed; $651M cash is the buffer.
  • 6.8x sales for a shrinking, loss-making business has no floor — sell, or hold only as a declared lottery ticket.
Sources BusinessWire — C3.ai Q1 FY2027 Business Insider — AA merger talks

bpost (bnode) BPOST.BR

Sell
  • No dividend for a second consecutive year.
  • Q3 2026: net loss €49.2m; FY2026 EBIT guidance cut again; mail volumes −16.4%; newspaper distribution in Wallonia ends 1 May 2027; press tax credit expires 31 Dec 2026 (extension blocked in parliament).
  • A structurally declining, politically dependent business — the clearest exit on the list.
Sources Quartr — bpost Q3 2026 La Libre — no dividend, 6 Mar 2026

Gaztransport & Technigaz GTT.PA

Add
  • Niche monopoly in LNG containment: order book 299 units / €1.8bn; 10-year demand forecast raised to ~550 ships.
  • Dividend +19% (FY2025) then +7.5% (FY2026 interim); ~80% payout; FY2026 guidance confirmed.
  • Genuinely independent of Belgium and of interest rates — the review's preferred home for new dividend capital.
Sources GTT Q3 2026 activity update PortNews — 10-year forecast
